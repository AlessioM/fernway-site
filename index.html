Run the forest.
Even when you don't.

Swipe between three lanes, leap rivers and pits, net butterflies by the dozen and knock orcs off the road. Spend your coins, ascend, and come back stronger — or put the phone down and let your hero keep running.

From footpath to legend

  1. 1

    Run

    Swipe up or down to change lane, tap to jump, swipe back to roll under bats, forward to dash. Hold to sweep butterflies into your net — the longer you hold, the bigger the multiplier.

  2. 2

    Upgrade

    Coins buy sharper blades, faster boots and a fatter purse. Every class has its own tree, and Instinct teaches your hero to dodge on their own.

  3. 3

    Ascend

    When the road gets long, ascend: trade your coins for Seeds, keep your class skills and plant permanent bonuses for every run that follows.

Everything on the road

Three heroes

The Knight hits hardest, the Rogue double-jumps and keeps the net bonus longest, the Wizard fires from afar. Run through a portal to switch mid-run.

Idle when you are

Upgrade Instinct and Wayfinder and your hero jumps, rolls and picks lanes alone. Back after a break? The forest kept a gift for you.

Net, combo, frenzy

Hold the net for up to ×2 coins, chain pickups into a combo, fill the meter and the whole road turns to gold in a frenzy.

Seeds & ascension

Start over richer: Seeds from all the coins you ever earned buy Golden Roots, Headstart, Sprouting and more — kept forever.

A forest that changes

Autumn groves, farms, rivers and cabins, then a stretch of snow with frosted pines, crystals and falling flakes. Stages get tougher — and pay better.

No account, plays offline

Just install and run — no sign-up, no login, no connection needed. Your progress is saved on your phone. Portrait or landscape, your choice.

Play it right here

The full game runs in any modern browser, on a computer or a phone. The first load is about 13 MB. Your progress is saved in this browser only, separate from the Android app.

  • Space jump

  • A roll

On a phone or with a mouse: swipe to steer, tap to jump, hold to net. Also on itch.io.
