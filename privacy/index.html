Fernway — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-14

Fernway ("the game") is developed by Classic Forest Games.

Data collection and sharing

The current release does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal or sensitive user data. It contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, user accounts, or developer-operated online service, and it does not request network access.

Your progress (coins, upgrades, Seeds, class experience) and settings are stored only on your device. The game does not send them to the developer or to a third party.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you reset progress in the game (Settings → Reset progress), clear the app's data, or uninstall the game. Because the developer never receives this data, there is no server-side copy to retrieve or delete.

Security

The game does not transmit user data over a network. Protect access to your device as usual.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated before any release that adds advertising, analytics, online services, or other data handling. The current version and effective date will remain available at the public privacy-policy URL listed on Google Play.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Classic Forest Games at classic.forest.games@gmail.com.
